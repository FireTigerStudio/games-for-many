## Our first filter: legal and safe distribution

A game must come from an official publisher program and provide an authorized embed. We do not copy game files or use unlicensed characters, names or artwork.

## What makes a good two-player browser game?

We favor fast loading, clear controls, a fair restart loop and genuine shared play. Local same-keyboard games are especially useful because two people can begin without account creation.

## How recommendations are tested

Each candidate is played on desktop, checked for broken controls and reviewed for adult, gambling, graphic violence and misleading ad behavior. Only approved entries become playable pages.
