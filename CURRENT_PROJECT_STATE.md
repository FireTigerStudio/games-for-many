## 1. Long-term objective

Build `gamesformany.com` into a safe, useful and commercially sustainable English browser-game site focused on reviewed two-player, local/shared-device, party and multiplayer games. Grow qualified Google traffic and advertising revenue through accurate inventory, useful player information, sound technical SEO and legitimate editorial links. Do not pursue rankings through unlicensed games, thin programmatic pages, purchased links, PBNs, school-circumvention/unblocked positioning or misleading multiplayer claims.

Success is not “rank first for everything.” The practical sequence is:

1. Get important URLs indexed and matched to the correct search intent.
2. Move already-visible queries from positions 8–20 into stable Top 10, then improve CTR and pursue Top 3 where evidence supports it.
3. Measure game starts and usable loads under valid consent.
4. Build topical authority and relevant referring domains around verified, differentiated page content.
5. Complete advertising/account prerequisites and optimize revenue only after traffic and UX are trustworthy.
